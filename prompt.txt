Write the single sentence a house cat would say in the five seconds after
sweeping a glass off the table -- at most 200 characters, in the cat's own
voice. No apology and no confession, just the most convincing case a cat can
make for itself.

The line must:
- be exactly ONE sentence, ONE line, at most 200 characters;
- sound like a cat's own logic, self-interest and total lack of remorse,
  never like a person apologising;
- land a FRESH comic idea -- avoid the obvious gravity, slippery-edge and
  it-was-already-falling lines entirely;
- fit five seconds: a spoken line a cat could plausibly deliver, not a
  written paragraph of reasoning;
- use every character well, with no padding or repeated joke.

Angles to prefer: the glass was at fault (it was empty, it was in the cat's
spot, it insulted the cat first), the cat as territorial owner of the table,
the cat as an official conducting business. The cat is arrogant, calm, and
convinced it did the right thing.

Deliverables in this repo: entry.txt holds the final line; main.py (stdlib
only, deterministic) prints it exactly and validates the gate with --check;
CANDIDATES.md holds ten candidate lines with the reasoning for the final pick.
